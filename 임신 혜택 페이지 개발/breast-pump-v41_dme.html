Common Questions

Is a breast pump really free through insurance? +
Yes! The Affordable Care Act (ACA) requires most insurance plans to cover a breast pump at no cost to you. We'll verify your specific coverage and show you exactly which pumps are fully covered.
When can I order my pump? +
Most insurance plans allow you to order 30–70 days before your due date. Some plans let us ship as soon as paperwork is complete. We'll let you know your exact shipping window.
What if I want an upgraded pump? +
You can apply your insurance benefit toward any pump — including wearable and upgraded models. You'll only pay the difference (upgrade fee), which is clearly shown before you order.
Can I get replacement parts covered too? +
Most insurance plans cover free replacement parts and milk storage bags every 30 days, starting 30 days after your baby's birth and continuing for up to one year. We'll remind you automatically!
